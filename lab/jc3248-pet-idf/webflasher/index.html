What gets flashed

The app image for your chosen pack at 0x10000 + the shared 3 MB LittleFS storage at 0x290000 (it holds all packs — only the boot default differs). ESP32-S3, JC3248W535 (320×480 AXS15231). Cats are CC0 — see each pack's PROVENANCE.md.
