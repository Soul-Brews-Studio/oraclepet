Firmware

Pick an app image: build/jc3248_pet_idf.bin or build/oraclepet_lcd28.bin. It is checked against the pet's board before anything is sent.

The pet writes the image to its idle slot, checks it, restarts into it and keeps the old one if the new one fails to start.

Animation pack

Pick a pack folder (manifest.json + GIFs), or a folder of packs such as lab/buddy/characters.

Pet app

A WebAssembly app built with lab/pet-sdk. Its file name becomes its name (a-z 0-9 - _).

Sound

The pet's mic (the 2.8in lcd28 board has one) records and measures what it hears.
Save .wav

Speech: the pet has no voice of its own; the host turns text into a clip. A browser cannot capture what its speech synthesis says, so this page cannot make the pet talk. OraclePet on the Mac can (or a host with espeak-ng or SAPI). A recording or a sound file works here.

Heartbeat

Sets the pet's state, clock and status line by hand. A heartbeat older than 30 s counts as idle.
